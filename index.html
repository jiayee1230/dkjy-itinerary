Arrival — Myeongdong

Wed, 27 May
Afternoon — Transit
Arrive Seoul via Hong Kong (CX410)
Two flights today — long journey. Check in to hotel, freshen up. Hotel is assumed near Myeongdong area based on nearby restaurants.
Hotel near Myeongdong
Dinner
Odarijip — Soy Marinated Crab (Ganjang Gejang)
2-minute walk from your hotel. Perfect first-night meal — no booking needed, easy on tired legs. Order the soy marinated crab over rice. 4.7 stars, 6,293 reviews. The roe mixed into rice is the highlight.
No reservation needed 2 min walk from hotel
Evening — Sightseeing
Myeongdong Shopping Street
Wander Myeongdong for skincare, street snacks, and first-night Seoul energy. Open 24h. Great for stocking up on sheet masks and trying hotteok.
Sightseeing

Anguk • Bukchon • Seochon • Gyeongbokgung

Thu, 28 May
7:00 AM — Breakfast
London Bagel Museum (Anguk branch)
Opens at 7am — go early to beat the queue. Download their app to join the virtual waitlist remotely while you explore the neighbourhood. Cheese honey bagel and jambon butter are crowd favourites. Queues can be 30–60 min on weekdays.
Opens 7am Queue expected — use their app
9:30 AM — Sightseeing
Bukchon Hanok Village
5-minute walk from London Bagel. Traditional hanok alleyways with sweeping city views. Best visited before 10am when the light is golden and crowds are thin. Residential area — keep voices down. Visit within 10am–5pm tourist window for best access.
10am–5pm tourist window
Mid-morning — Snack
Dotori Garden Anguk — Greek Yogurt Bowl
Right across from Bukchon. Famous honey yogurt bowl served in a whimsical cafe playing Ghibli music. Go for the acai or honey variant. Salt bread here is also excellent. Opens 8am. Can get crowded by 10am.
Cafe • Opens 8am
11:00 AM — Sightseeing
Gyeongbokgung Palace
10-minute walk. 15th-century Joseon Dynasty palace. Rent a hanbok at the entrance for free admission. Allow 2–3 hours to explore. The changing of the guard ceremony is worth timing your visit around. Closed Tuesdays — you are going Thursday so it's open.
Open 9am–5pm • Closed Tuesdays
2:00 PM — Sightseeing + Snack
Seochon Hanok Village + JAISO SEOCHON Daifuku
Just west of Gyeongbokgung. Quieter and more residential than Bukchon — fewer tourists, more charm. Grab seasonal mochi (strawberry or persimmon) from JAISO, a tiny dessert shop with 4.9 stars. Opens 11am Wed–Sun, closed Mon–Tue.
Neighbourhood stroll Daifuku mochi • Opens 11am
Dinner
Gobongdang — Spiced Pork Ribs with Rice Cake
Incredibly rated (4.9 stars, 555 reviews) for juicy spiced ribs and chewy rice cake. Near Hyehwa / Daehakro area, about 20 minutes from Seochon. Book in advance — the injeolmi-style grilled rice cake is unique and unforgettable. Opens noon daily.
Dinner • Bookable 🔔 Reserve before your trip!
⚠ Book this restaurant online before you fly. Once your itinerary is confirmed, secure a table immediately.

Hongdae • Mangwon • Yeonnam

Fri, 29 May
9:00 AM — Breakfast
Salt Bread
Light, buttery salt bread to kick off the day. Opens at 9am sharp so plan accordingly. A quick and satisfying breakfast before heading to the market.
Opens 9am sharp
10:00 AM — Market
Mangwon Market
A beloved local neighbourhood market — very different energy from touristy Gwangjang. Great for street food, kimchi, fresh produce, and soaking in local life. The stir-fried rice cake stalls are excellent. Go earlier on weekdays to beat the crowd. Opens 9am.
Local market • Opens 9am
11:30 AM — Snack
Ugly Bakery — Matcha Mammoth Bread
Near Mangwon. Opens noon Wed–Sun. Queue 15 minutes before opening as crowds form fast. The mammoth breads (pistachio, matcha) are stuffed with thick custard and whole nuts — unique and genuinely delicious. Small local market nearby sells gimbap while you wait.
Opens noon • Queue up Closed Mon–Tue
Lunch
GwangGye — Innovative Ginseng Chicken Soup (Samgyetang)
In Yeonnam-dong, opposite Mandong Bakery. A modern take with perilla or black sesame broth — lighter and less medicinal than traditional samgyetang. Perfect 5.0 star rating. Closed Wednesdays. Opens noon.
Lunch • Yeonnam-dong • Opens noon
After lunch — Snack
Mandong Bakery — Garlic Loaf Bread
Right next to GwangGye in Yeonnam-dong. Famous garlic bread with a crispy exterior and soft, fluffy interior. Staff often give out samples. Opens 11am. Grab one to go — it travels well.
Bakery • Opens 11am
Afternoon — Sightseeing
Hongdae Street
Street performers, indie shops, quirky cafes, and youthful energy. Best explored on foot in the afternoon. Great light for photos and lots to discover down side streets. Live music performances happen throughout the evening.
Free roam
Dinner
Ilpyeon Eel Hongdae — Grilled Eel (Jangeo)
2nd floor in Hongdae. Fresh live eel grilled tableside — staff show you the eels before cooking and demonstrate how to eat it properly. Flying fish roe rice is a must-add to the set. 4.9 stars, 3,889 reviews. Bookable. Opens noon. Fri–Sat until midnight.
Dinner • Bookable
After dinner — Dessert
Ichi Seoul — May Seasonal Parfait (Tomato Melon)
In the Hongdae area — easy to combine after dinner. The May seasonal parfait features tomato and melon. Check if still available when you're there.
Seasonal parfait

Gwangjang • Insadong • Euljiro • Namsan

Sat, 30 May
8:00 AM — Breakfast
Toast (Korean Egg Toast)
Light, affordable Korean toast to start the day before hitting the market. Quick and satisfying.
Breakfast
9:30 AM — Market
Gwangjang Market
Seoul's iconic traditional market — bindaetteok (mung bean pancakes), mayak gimbap, and fabric stalls. Several Michelin-recommended stalls inside. Opens 9am. Explore the food hall before it gets packed by mid-morning. The Netflix-famous kalguksu is here too.
Market • Opens 9am
11:30 AM — Sightseeing + Market
Namdaemun Market + Insadong
Namdaemun opens 9am (closed Sundays) — good for souvenirs and the famous sugar-cinnamon hotteok. Walk through Insadong for art galleries, independent tea houses, and traditional crafts.
Namdaemun • Closed Sundays Insadong
Lunch
Hijukhijuk — Abalone Porridge + Abalone Chicken Soup
7-minute walk from your hotel, in the Myeongdong area. Opens 7am. Silky-smooth abalone porridge with generous fresh abalone. The seafood pancake pairs perfectly. Very foreigner-friendly staff. Can also double as breakfast on any day.
Lunch • Near hotel • Opens 7am
3:30 PM — Sightseeing
N Seoul Tower — Sunset Views
Take the cable car up Namsan mountain. Aim to arrive around 5–6pm for golden hour and sunset views over the city. Hiking up is also an option (20–30 min, well-marked trail). Open from 10:30am. The view at dusk is unforgettable.
Aim for sunset • Opens 10:30am
Dinner
Eulji Grilled Skirt Meat — Signature Pork Diaphragm BBQ
In the Euljiro area. Cannot be booked — arrive before 6pm or expect a significant queue, especially on a Saturday. Their signature pork diaphragm (skirt meat) is the reason regulars keep returning. A true local BBQ experience with no tourist-friendly frills.
BBQ DinnerNo reservation — arrive early!
⚠ Saturday night will be busy. Aim to arrive by 5:30pm to get a table without a long wait.

Michelin Guide • Opens 10:50am

Strawberry Milk + Mint Ice Cream
Two dessert stops to combine in the afternoon — stroll between them and enjoy. The 32cm soft-serve at the Hongdae area parfait shop is also worth grabbing (only 3,000 KRW for an enormous cone).
Dessert crawl

4.9 stars, 329 reviews. Aged pork cooked tableside by attentive staff who speak English. The watercress (su-chae) wrapping gives this BBQ its distinctive flavour. Bookable. Open until 2am on Sundays — no rush on timing.

Practical Tips
🚌 Get a T-money card from any convenience store (7-Eleven, GS25) at the airport — works on all buses, metro lines, and even some taxis.
✈ AREX express train from Seoul Station to Incheon Airport takes ~43 min. Allow 2.5 hours before your CX427 departure.
📷 Bukchon and Gyeongbokgung are best photographed in the morning before tour groups arrive. Go early, then eat breakfast after.
📜 Naver Maps is more accurate than Google Maps in Seoul. Download it before your trip — most restaurant links in your list are Naver links.
💰 Korea is still largely cash-friendly. Have some Korean Won (KRW) for markets — Gwangjang and Mangwon vendors may not accept cards.
🍔 Gyeongbokgung is closed on Tuesdays. Namdaemun Market is closed on Sundays. Plan accordingly.
🌞 Late May in Seoul is warm (22–28°C) with occasional rain. Pack a light layer and a compact umbrella.
